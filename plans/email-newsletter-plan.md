## ⚠️ Very Important Rules

### ✅ What you SHOULD do:
1.  ✅ **80% Value / 20% Promotion** rule always
2.  ✅ Always give useful practical information
3.  ✅ Keep emails short and easy to read
4.  ✅ Always invite to Telegram channel at the end
5.  ✅ Write like you are talking directly to them

### ❌ What you SHOULD NEVER do:
1.  ❌ Never sell directly in first 3 emails
2.  ❌ Never send more than 1 email per week
3.  ❌ Never send spam or copied content
4.  ❌ Never use clickbait titles
5.  ❌ Never break trust once it's built
