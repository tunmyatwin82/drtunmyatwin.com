## ⚠️ Important Principles

### ❌ What NOT to do:
- Don't add everything at once
- Don't overcrowd the landing page
- Don't sell on first visit
- Don't break the existing good conversion flow

### ✅ What you SHOULD do:
1.  Add one feature at a time
2.  Test each feature for 1-2 weeks
3.  Only keep what works
4.  Always keep the landing page clean and focused on the main offer
